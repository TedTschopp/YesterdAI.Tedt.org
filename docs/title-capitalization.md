# Title and Heading Capitalization

Ted selected the [linked AP-style title-case rules](https://headlinecapitalization.com/rules/ap-style-title-case/) on October 24, 2026 as Yesterday in AI's house style. This policy supersedes sentence-case headline guidance in the Edison writing guide and the design skill for this project.

## Where It Applies

Use title case for publication-authored HTML h1–h6, Markdown headings, article and edition titles, page titles, topic names, and section or sidebar headers, including headers styled as labels. Search results, contents links, feeds, and social/search metadata share the same authored titles. Keep ordinary sentences, descriptions, summaries, captions, form instructions, and action links in sentence case.

## Capitalization Rules

- Capitalize the first and last words.
- Capitalize nouns, verbs (including Is, Be, and Do), adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, and subordinating conjunctions.
- Capitalize words of four or more letters, including With, From, Into, and Over.
- Lowercase articles, coordinating conjunctions, and prepositions of three or fewer letters unless they are first or last: a, an, the, and, but, or, for, at, by, in, of, on, to.
- Keep infinitive to lowercase. Capitalize particles in phrasal verbs, such as Give Up.
- In hyphenated compounds, capitalize major parts; short minor parts stay lowercase.
- After a colon, capitalize a word according to these rules; capitalize a short minor word if it begins a complete sentence.

## Preserve Names and Links

Keep official spelling such as OpenAI, LiteLLM, arXiv, MiniMax, ChatGPT, and AI, along with code identifiers and the publication name Yesterday in AI. Quoted source titles and upstream feed records retain their supplied spelling and capitalization.

Review capitalization in the authored text. CSS text-transform and blanket lowercasing cannot express these rules reliably. Preserve stable IDs, slugs, URLs, explicit heading anchors, attribution, and publication dates; record article or edition revisions with modified_at. Keep existing heading links working when changing capitalization.

Examples: Microsoft Expands AI Tools for Utility Workers; How to Work With AI; The Best Ticket-Routing Decision May Be to Defer.
