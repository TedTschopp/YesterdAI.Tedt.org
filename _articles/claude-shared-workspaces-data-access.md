A colleague can open a shared Claude Data dashboard and see fresh numbers even without access to the connector that retrieves them. Anthropic says the live-sharing beta lets the author approve the queries that supply those numbers. The dashboard’s sharing settings determine who can see the results. [Anthropic’s artifact announcement](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)

Anthropic began rolling out Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards in beta on Nov. 10. Sheets lets teams and Claude edit cells and formulas in a shared spreadsheet. Whiteboards gives them a shared canvas for arranging ideas and drafting actions from notes. Claude Data, previously called Claude Dashboards, is now generally available. Certified queries, which let a data team approve a common definition for a metric, are still in beta alongside live sharing. The company also announced interactive connectors that can open a partner app inside Claude. It described Claude Meetings as coming soon. [Anthropic’s rollout details](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors)

The settings give administrators a specific date to work with. Anthropic says all three artifacts start on for Team plans. On Enterprise plans, Sheets and Whiteboards start off, and Claude Data turns on by default Nov. 19. An administrator can change those choices in Organization settings under Artifacts. [Anthropic’s plan settings](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)

Try the sharing decision with an ordinary viewer account before using a sensitive data source. A staffing dashboard might share headcount by department with a leadership group. The owner can inspect the query result for employee-level detail and confirm who can open the dashboard. That is the result the group will receive, regardless of who can sign in to the data connector.
